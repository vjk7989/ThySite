# Buckleson domain glossary

- **Buckleson:** Company brand for AI trust and execution infrastructure.
- **Hyper Tern:** Policy-aware routing and permission control around AI requests.
- **Hyper-ABS:** Task-aware data transformation designed to reduce unnecessary raw-data exposure before inference.
- **Hyper-0x:** Buckleson’s in-house verification layer for attributable, tamper-evident execution records.
- **Hyper Wallet:** Secondary identity/signing interface for declared intent and execution receipts.
- **Control boundary:** The enforceable point where identity, intent, scope, data, capability, and evidence policy is applied.
- **Execution record:** Evidence that an action occurred with defined context; it does not prove the action was safe or correct.
- **Assessment:** A review of one bounded AI workflow to locate risks, enforcement points, required evidence, and operating owners.
