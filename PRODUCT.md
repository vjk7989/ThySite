# Buckleson Product Context

Buckleson helps organizations, AI teams, developers, and individuals use AI more safely. Hyper-0x is Buckleson's in-house trust and execution layer for AI infrastructure.

## Audience and outcome

- Primary audience: mixed technical and business decision-makers evaluating AI safety, deployment, and governance.
- Primary outcome: understand the control boundaries and request an AI safety assessment.
- Primary call to action: **Request an AI Safety Assessment**.

## Product structure

- **Hyper Tern:** routing and permission controls between models and infrastructure.
- **Hyper-ABS:** data abstraction and privacy-preserving transformations before inference.
- **Hyper-0x:** verifiable execution and audit records using Buckleson's blockchain layer.
- **Hyper Wallet:** secondary proof of blockchain implementation capability, not a primary navigation item.

## Voice and claims

The voice is trusted technical: authoritative, transparent, precise, and restrained-futuristic. Describe controls as designed to reduce risk or exposure. Never claim that Buckleson eliminates every AI risk, guarantees privacy or compliance, or makes AI inherently safe through blockchain.
