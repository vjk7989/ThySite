Buckleson helps organizations adopt AI more safely by mapping one workflow at a time: who can invoke it, what data it can use, which model or service route is allowed, what tools it may reach, and what evidence should remain for review.

Hyper-0x is Buckleson's in-house trust layer for AI infrastructure. It is designed to make execution more accountable; it does not make model output inherently correct, guarantee privacy, or remove the need for conventional security controls.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Control with Hyper Tern" icon="setting">
    Mediate model, tool, and infrastructure access through explicit routing,
    permission, and human-review boundaries.
  </Card>

<Card title="Protect with Hyper-ABS" icon="shield">
  Minimize or transform sensitive information before inference to help reduce
  unnecessary raw-data exposure.
</Card>

  <Card title="Verify with Hyper-0x" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Preserve attributable execution records for audit, incident review, and
    operational learning.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

The platform addresses specific control, privacy, and auditability concerns. Additional controls remain necessary for prompt injection, hallucinations, supply-chain integrity, resource limits, sandboxing, model evaluation, and human review.
